Methodology — short
Each signal is produced using the Structured Clarity Systems Framework:
  • Behavioral patterns: funding, leverage shifts, derivatives flow
  • On-chain flows: exchange inflows/outflows, whale wallets, accumulation
  • Narrative momentum: social sentiment & news cycle inflection
Signals are timestamped (public post) before outcomes occur. Outcomes validated against objective criteria documented per entry.
